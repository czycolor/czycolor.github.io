ForChina Uploader

Application overview · 应用说明

What this app does

ForChina Uploader is a private, personal-use desktop tool. Its single purpose is to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel (ForChina, @forchina-j7m) through the official YouTube Data API. It is used exclusively by its developer/owner; it is not distributed to other users, and it does not collect, store, share or sell any third-party user data.

本应用用途

「ForChina Uploader」为私人自用桌面工具，唯一用途是通过 YouTube 官方 Data API 向所有者自己的 YouTube 频道（ForChina，@forchina-j7m）上传和管理视频。该应用不分发给其他用户， 不收集、不存储、不共享任何第三方用户数据。
